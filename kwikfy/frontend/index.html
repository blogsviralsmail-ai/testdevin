FAQ

Everything you wanted to ask

Do I need to switch my store or replace Shopify?

No. Kwikfy sits on top of your existing setup. Connect your Shopify store, WhatsApp number, couriers and payment gateway — Kwikfy unifies them into one dashboard without replacing anything.

Is the WhatsApp integration official and approved?

Yes. Kwikfy uses the official WhatsApp Business Platform (Cloud API) via Meta. You connect your own WhatsApp Business number through Meta's secure Embedded Signup — no unofficial workarounds.

How does the RTO / COD-return reduction work?

Kwikfy analyses your order and courier data to flag high-risk COD orders before they ship, and can automatically offer those customers a prepaid option over WhatsApp — helping you cut return losses instead of writing them off.

Is my data safe and separate from other brands?

Every brand gets its own isolated database. We use access controls, encryption in transit (HTTPS) and server-side secret storage. Your data is yours — export or delete it anytime.

Are GST reports really built in?

Yes. Kwikfy generates GST-ready reports with HSN mapping and CGST/SGST/IGST breakdowns, alongside your live P&L and reconciliation — so your finance work is done inside the same dashboard.

Is there a contract or lock-in?

No lock-in. Start free, upgrade as you grow, and cancel anytime from your dashboard. Your plan stays active until the end of the period you've paid for.
