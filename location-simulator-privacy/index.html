Privacy Policy — Location Simulator Pro

Effective date: 20 April 2026 · Contact: blogsviralsmail@gmail.com

This Privacy Policy explains how the Android application Location Simulator Pro (package com.blogsvirals.locationsimulator, published by Blogs Virals, hereafter “we”, “us”, or “the app”) handles your data.

1. Summary

  • The app is designed for developers, QA engineers and power users who want to simulate GPS location for testing and educational purposes.
  • We do not sell your data.
  • Location data that you configure inside the app stays on your device.
  • The only network calls the app makes are to public mapping services (OpenStreetMap tiles, Nominatim search and OSRM routing) for addresses and routes you explicitly request.
  • If you enable Firebase Analytics / Crashlytics (shipped disabled by default for new accounts), anonymous crash and usage statistics may be sent to Google.

2. Data we collect

2.1 Data stored on your device only

  • Saved places (name, latitude, longitude, optional notes).
  • Profiles (name, default speed, default location).
  • Simulation history for the last 30 days (mode, timestamp, coordinates, speed).
  • App preferences (PIN hash for App Lock, UI theme settings).

This data is written to the app’s private Room SQLite database and DataStore preferences file inside Android’s sandbox. It is never uploaded to our servers.

2.2 Data sent to third-party mapping services

When you search for an address, pick a destination or compute a driving route, the app sends the query or coordinates to the following free public services:

  • Nominatim (OpenStreetMap) — usage policy.
  • OSRM router — router.project-osrm.org.
  • OSM tile servers for map imagery.

These services may log standard web request metadata (IP address, user agent, query) according to their own privacy policies. We do not operate these services.

2.3 Firebase (optional)

The app ships with the Firebase SDK so that developers can later enable:

  • Analytics — anonymous usage counters.
  • Crashlytics — anonymized crash reports (stack trace, device model, OS version). No personal data is included.
  • Remote Config — fetches feature flags from Google.

In the v1.0.0 default build these are disabled in code unless you link a real google-services.json. When enabled, the data handling follows Firebase’s privacy documentation.

2.4 AdMob (optional)

The app includes Google AdMob to optionally show non-personalised banner advertisements. AdMob may collect advertising identifiers, coarse location and device information as described in Google’s AdMob policy. Ads are turned off in the v1.0.0 build by default; the publisher may enable them in a later update.

3. How we use the data

  • To render the map, search results and routes you requested.
  • To let you recall saved places / profiles and your 30-day simulation history.
  • To keep your app locked with a PIN if you enabled App Lock (the PIN is stored as an irreversible SHA-256 hash).

4. Permissions

The app requests the following Android permissions:

  • ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION & ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION — required to register as a mock location provider.
  • ACCESS_BACKGROUND_LOCATION & FOREGROUND_SERVICE_LOCATION — so the simulation keeps running when the screen is off, under a persistent notification.
  • ACCESS_MOCK_LOCATION — to provide fake locations via Android’s standard mock provider API.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS — to show the foreground-service notification.
  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE — for map tiles, search and routing.
  • SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW — to draw the floating joystick overlay.
  • USE_BIOMETRIC — optional biometric unlock for App Lock.
  • VIBRATE — haptic feedback on joystick/teleport.

5. Data retention & deletion

  • Saved places and profiles remain until you delete them or uninstall the app.
  • Simulation history auto-expires at 30 days; you can also clear it any time from Settings → Clear history.
  • Uninstalling the app removes all locally-stored data.
  • If Firebase is enabled, you can request deletion of your Crashlytics data by emailing us at blogsviralsmail@gmail.com.

6. Children’s privacy

The app is intended for users aged 18 and above. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13 (or 16 in the EEA). If you believe a child has submitted information, contact us and we will delete it.

7. Security

All network traffic uses HTTPS. The local database lives in Android’s private app storage. PINs for the App Lock feature are stored as one-way SHA-256 hashes and never leave the device. However, no mobile app can be guaranteed 100% secure.

8. Changes to this policy

If we materially change this policy we will update the “Effective date” above and announce the change in the Play Store release notes.
