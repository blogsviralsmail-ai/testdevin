Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

1. Introduction

KKHS Media ("we", "our", "us") is a YouTube video management dashboard hosted at kkhsmedia.com. This privacy policy explains how we handle your data when you use our service.

2. Data We Access

When you connect your YouTube account, we access:

  • Your YouTube channel information (name, subscriber count, video count)
  • Your YouTube video metadata and statistics
  • YouTube Analytics data (views, watch time, demographics)
  • Ability to upload videos on your behalf

3. How We Use Your Data

Your YouTube data is used solely to:

  • Display your channel information and analytics on the dashboard
  • Upload and schedule videos to your YouTube channel
  • Show video performance metrics

4. Data Storage

OAuth tokens are stored securely on our server with restricted file permissions. We do not share, sell, or distribute your data to any third parties. Video files uploaded through our dashboard are stored temporarily on our server and are uploaded directly to YouTube.

5. Data Retention

You can disconnect your YouTube account at any time through the dashboard, which immediately deletes all stored tokens and access credentials.

6. Third-Party Services

We use the YouTube Data API v3 and YouTube Analytics API v2. Your use of these services is also governed by YouTube's Terms of Service and Google's Privacy Policy.

7. Security

We implement security measures including SSL/TLS encryption, password-protected dashboard access, and restricted file permissions for sensitive data.

8. Contact

For privacy questions, contact us at: support@kkhsmedia.com
