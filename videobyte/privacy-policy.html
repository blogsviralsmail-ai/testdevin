Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 May 2026

KKHS Media ("we", "our", "the Service"), operated by KKHS Media, helps creators publish and schedule video content to their own connected social media accounts (YouTube, Facebook Pages, and Instagram). This policy explains what we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account info: your name and email when you sign in to KKHS Media.
  • Connected platform data (with your authorization):
    • Google / YouTube: your channel ID, channel name, public statistics, video metadata, and analytics — accessed only after you grant consent via Google OAuth.
    • Meta / Facebook & Instagram: your Facebook Page ID and name, the linked Instagram Business account ID, and tokens needed to publish content you create — accessed only after you grant consent via Facebook Login.
  • Content you provide: videos, images, titles, descriptions, tags, and schedules that you choose to publish through KKHS Media.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To publish and schedule the content you create to the accounts you connect.
  • To show you analytics and reports for your own channels and posts.
  • To operate, secure, and maintain the Service.

We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or share it with third parties for their own purposes.

3. Google API Services — Limited Use

KKHS Media's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. We use Google/YouTube data solely to provide the upload, scheduling, and analytics features you request, and never for serving ads or for resale.

4. Meta Platform

Our use of Facebook and Instagram data complies with the Meta Platform Terms and Developer Policies. We use Page and Instagram permissions only to publish the content you explicitly choose to post, and to display your own post performance.

5. Data Storage & Security

Access tokens are stored securely on our servers with restricted permissions and are used only to perform the actions you request. Data is transmitted over HTTPS. We retain connection data only while your account is connected.

6. Data Retention & Deletion

You may disconnect any account at any time from KKHS Media's Settings, which immediately removes the stored access tokens for that account. To delete all of your data, see our Data Deletion page or email support@kkhsmedia.com. You may also revoke access directly via your Google Account or Facebook Business Integrations settings.

7. Children's Privacy

KKHS Media is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

8. Changes

We may update this policy; material changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date above.
