Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: 31 May 2026

You can delete the data KKHS Media holds about you at any time. We only store what is needed to publish to the accounts you connect.

Option 1 — Disconnect from KKHS Media (instant)

  • Sign in to KKHS Media and open Settings → Connected Accounts.
  • Click Disconnect next to any YouTube channel, Facebook Page, or Instagram account.
  • This immediately deletes the stored access tokens for that account from our servers.

Option 2 — Revoke from the platform

  • Google/YouTube: myaccount.google.com/permissions → remove KKHS Media.
  • Facebook/Instagram: Facebook → Business Integrations → remove KKHS Media.

Option 3 — Request full deletion

Email support@kkhsmedia.com from your registered email with the subject "Delete my data". We will permanently delete all data associated with your account within 7 days and confirm by email.

After deletion, KKHS Media retains no access tokens, connection data, or analytics for the removed accounts.
