Privacy Policy

CallPro: Call Log Editor, Backup & Dialer

Last updated: June 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how KKHS Media Private Limited ("we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use the CallPro app ("the App").

1. Information We Access

The App requires the following permissions to function:

  • Call Log (Read & Write) - To display, edit, add, and delete call history entries on your device. This is the core functionality of the App.
  • Contacts (Read) - To display your contacts list and show contact names in call history.
  • Phone (Call) - To make phone calls directly from the App's dialer.
  • Phone State (Read) - To detect incoming and outgoing calls for the in-call screen.

2. Data Storage

All data processed by the App is stored locally on your device only. This includes:

  • Call log entries (read from and written to Android's system call log)
  • App preferences (theme, app lock settings)
  • Backup files (stored in local device storage)
  • Device identifier (used solely for subscription tracking)

We do NOT collect, transmit, or store any of your data on external servers.

3. Data We Do NOT Collect

  • We do NOT collect personal information
  • We do NOT record or access call audio
  • We do NOT access your camera, microphone, or location
  • We do NOT share any data with third parties
  • We do NOT use analytics or tracking services
  • We do NOT display ads

4. In-App Purchases

The App offers premium features via Google Play In-App Purchases. Payment processing is handled entirely by Google Play. We do not have access to your payment information (credit card, UPI details, etc.).

5. Device Identifier

The App generates a unique device identifier (based on Android ID and device hardware information) solely for the purpose of tracking your subscription status locally. This identifier is stored only on your device and is never transmitted to any server.

6. Backup & Export

When you create a backup or export your call logs, the files are saved locally on your device. You have full control over these files and can share or delete them as you wish. We do not have access to these files.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected in the "Last updated" date above.
