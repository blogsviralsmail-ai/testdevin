APP NAME: CallPro: Call Log Editor, Backup & Dialer

------------------------------------
SHORT DESCRIPTION (80 chars max):
------------------------------------

------------------------------------
FULL DESCRIPTION:
------------------------------------
CallPro is your all-in-one call management app — a smart dialer with powerful call log editing, backup, and organization features.

KEY FEATURES:

-- Call Log Editor --
- Edit any call log entry — change number, contact name, date, time, and duration
- Add new call log entries (incoming, outgoing, missed, rejected)
- Delete individual or all call logs
- Support for all call types including Voicemail and Blocked

-- Smart Dialer --
- Full-featured dialer with T9 keypad
- Quick call from contacts or call history
- Set as default phone app
- Incoming & outgoing call screen

-- Contacts --
- Browse all your contacts with search
- Tap to call any contact instantly
- Contact names displayed in call history

-- Backup & Restore --
- Backup entire call history to JSON file
- Restore from backup anytime
- Export call logs to CSV or JSON format
- Share exported files easily

-- Call Analytics --
- View call statistics and patterns
- Most called contacts
- Total call duration and averages
- Calls by day and hour analysis

-- Security --
- App Lock with 4-digit PIN
- Protect your call history from unauthorized access

-- Customization --
- Dark and Light theme support
- Clean Material Design 3 interface

WHY CALLPRO?
- 100% offline — no internet required, no data collection
- No ads — clean, distraction-free experience
- Lightweight — minimal storage and battery usage
- Privacy focused — all data stays on your device

PERMISSIONS EXPLAINED:
- Call Log: Required to read, edit, add, and delete call history
- Contacts: Required to display your contacts list
- Phone: Required for the dialer to make calls
- Phone State: Required for incoming call detection
